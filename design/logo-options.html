Twin-sun horizon — five marks

The current mark (top-left) is two equal, overlapping discs side by side: the Mastercard silhouette. Every real twin-sun image in your screenshots does the opposite — different sizes, different heights, never touching, a horizon doing the work. The options below keep the brief (two suns, red + orange, horizon, black) and change only the geometry. Each shown at favicon, nav and hero size, plus the wordmark and a light-surface version.
